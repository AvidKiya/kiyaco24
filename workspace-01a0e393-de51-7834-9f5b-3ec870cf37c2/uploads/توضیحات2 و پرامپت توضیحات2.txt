Previous iterations became visually overloaded because inverted corners were used everywhere.
Avoid that.
Use inverted corners strategically for:
Hero sections

Featured cards
Editorial sections
Collection sections
Special CTA areas
Product campaign blocks
Not every card should have an inverted corner.

5. IMPORTANT — USE THE EMPTY SPACE
This is one of the most important design requirements.
When an inverted corner creates an empty / recessed area, DO NOT leave it useless.
Use the recessed space intentionally.
Examples:
CTA button
Floating button
Mini promotional banner
Product badge
Discount badge
Arrow
"View Collection" action
"Shop Now" action
Editorial label
Category shortcut
Decorative-but-functional control
Example concept:
A large editorial card has a concave corner.
Instead of leaving the corner empty:
Place a floating CTA inside or partially overlapping the recessed area.
The empty space created by the inverted corner should become a FUNCTIONAL DESIGN AREA.
However:
Keep it subtle.
The purpose is to create a distinctive visual identity, not visual chaos.
