You are a senior product designer, UX/UI designer, e-commerce architect, full-stack developer, SEO specialist, CRM strategist, and fashion-commerce consultant.

Your task is to DESIGN AND BUILD a complete, modern, premium, scalable, production-ready e-commerce website for a fashion accessories brand specializing in:

* Women's belts
* Fashion accessories
* Costume jewelry / fashion jewelry
* Jewelry sets
* Related fashion products

The website must NOT look like a generic e-commerce template.

It should feel like a premium fashion brand combined with a modern editorial fashion magazine and a professional B2B wholesale platform.

The PRIMARY PURPOSE of the website is ONLINE SHOPPING.

The website must have three major pillars:

1. B2C RETAIL SHOPPING — primary business
2. B2B WHOLESALE SHOPPING — for retailers and business partners
3. FASHION DAILY — a daily digital fashion newspaper / magazine containing fashion news, trends, styling, runway news, popular products, colors, celebrity styles, seasonal trends, and editorial content

The content system must ultimately support commerce:
CONTENT → DISCOVERY → PRODUCT → PURCHASE

Do not sacrifice shopping UX for editorial content.
